# Task 37's scenario: a three-sorcerer party whose way through the start
# area (AR1000) ends with twelve spells cast at one goblin, to exercise
# the projectile and effect rows (CProjectile, IcewindCVisualEffect).
#
# The party is produced by the scenario itself, but not through the cheat
# console: the console's edit control never processes Enter in our engine
# (CUIControlEdit::OnEditReturn is empty; docs/followups.md), so no input
# sequence can type into it. The console being unreachable was ruled on
# and the equivalent is what first_fight.txt already proved: character
# generation, where a sorcerer's four spell picks make the quickslots
# that the Cast Spell bar shows. Three characters, Aria, Bram and Cade,
# are generated here (Elf, Sorcerer, Chaotic Good, the same abilities,
# skills and feat as first_fight's Probe), each with four different
# spells: twelve of the eighteen level-1 wizard/sorcerer spells in all.
#
# The walk to the fight is first_fight.txt's, verbatim, with Aria as the
# leader. Seed 1 (from first_fight): the goblin (1000_Goblin_01, 10GOB,
# 4 hit points) at 511,763 comes into view on leg 6 (Enemy Sighted
# auto-pause), the party stops at 519,902 after Space and the goblin
# comes to 504,882 to attack, its first attack auto-pausing the game
# (Attacked, 0x2). Centred on Aria (view 119,686) she is at screen
# 400,216 and the goblin at 385,196.
#
# While paused, twelve casts are given: the Cast Spell bar (179,463; the
# bar's buttons at x 27 + 38 * n), each sorcerer's spells in the order
# they were picked in (the quickslots keep pick order), then a target.
# The target comes from the spell's ability actionType (SPELL_ABILITY
# +0x0C, IcewindMisc::CreateButtonData): 1 targets a creature (click the
# goblin at 390,188, or key 1 to 3 for a party member), 4 targets ground
# (click north of the party at 386,120: world 505,806, away from the
# party for Grease's and Sleep's areas and their radius, Sleep further
# out at 386,90: world 505,776), and 5 needs no target (the slot itself
# fires). A caster's portrait key is pressed once per switch only: the
# key of an already-solely-selected member is a portrait double-click
# and opens the character record instead.
#
# Space ends the pause and the queue plays out: Magic Missile and Ice
# Dagger fly at the goblin (1d4+1 and 1d4 damage on 4 hit points), the
# cones and areas land north of the party, the self and friend spells
# (Shield, Minor Mirror Image, Mage Armor, Eagle's Splendor, Larloch's
# Minor Drain, Charm Person) land on the casters and the goblin. The
# rolls (damage, Grease slips, Charm) are past the pass gate: only
# experience points and enemy hit points are compared.
#
