# Task 37's scenario: a three-sorcerer party whose way through the start
# area (AR1000) ends with ten spells cast - projectiles at the goblin,
# cones and areas on the ground north of the party - to exercise the
# projectile and effect rows (CProjectile, IcewindCVisualEffect).
#
# The party is produced by the scenario itself, but not through the cheat
# console: the console's edit control never processes Enter in our engine
# (CUIControlEdit::OnEditReturn is empty; docs/followups.md), so no input
# sequence can type into it. The console being unreachable was ruled on
# and the equivalent is what first_fight.txt already proved: character
# generation, where a sorcerer's four spell picks make the quickslots
# that the Cast Spell bar shows. Three characters, Aria, Bram and Cade,
# are generated here (Elf, Sorcerer, Chaotic Good, the same abilities,
# skills and feat as first_fight's Probe), each with four different
# spells: twelve of the eighteen level-1 wizard/sorcerer spells in all.
#
# The walk to the fight is first_fight.txt's, verbatim, with Aria as the
# leader. Seed 1 (from first_fight): the goblin (1000_Goblin_01, 10GOB,
# 4 hit points) at 511,763 comes into view on leg 6 (Enemy Sighted
# auto-pause), the party stops at 519,902 after Space and the goblin
# comes to 504,882 to attack, its first attack auto-pausing the game
# (Attacked, 0x2). Centred on Aria (view 119,686) she is at screen
# 400,216 and the goblin at 385,196.
#
# While paused, ten casts are given with keys alone: a character key
# (1 to 3) selects that sorcerer alone (CScreenWorld::OnKeyDown also
# swaps the action bar to her slots), F5 is the action bar's fifth
# button (OnLButtonPressed(4): customizable[1] = 3 = Cast Spell on the
# default sorcerer bar, CInfButtonArray.cpp:95), and F1 to F4 pick from
# the cast list that opens (CInfButtonArray state 103; the list keeps
# chargen pick order - the picks are appended as they are clicked - and
# the F-key index is the list index, CInfButtonArray.cpp:3075). Each
# cast that takes a valid target fires and the bar returns to state 0,
# so the next character key is a clean select. The target comes from
# the spell's ability actionType (SPELL_ABILITY +0x0C, IcewindMisc::
# CreateButtonData): 1 targets a creature (click it, or a character key
# while armed to target that party member - CScreenWorld.cpp:880 picks
# the target instead of selecting when a spell is armed), 4 targets
# ground (click north of the party at 386,120, away from the party for
# the cones' and areas' radius, Sleep further out at 386,90), and 5
# needs no target. None of the ten is actionType 5.
#
# Bram casts first, all four of his: Ray of Enfeeblement at the goblin
# (strength drain, no hit-point damage, so the goblin's 4 hit points
# stay for the damage casts), Ice Dagger (1d4) and Magic Missile
# (1d4+1) at it - 2 + 2 damage at the least, so it drops here - and
# Color Spray north. Aria's and Cade's creature casts target party
# members by character key instead: Bram is done casting, so his hit
# points are free to spend, and a party member is always a valid
# creature target. That also avoids the one trap the route has: a cast
# canceled for an invalid target stays armed, and the next character
# key would then target that member with the armed spell. Larloch's
# Minor Drain (1d4) and Chromatic Orb (1d8) land on Bram, Cade's
# second Ray (no damage) on Aria, who is done casting by then. The
# ground casts are Color Spray, Burning Hands and Grease at 386,120
# and Sleep at 386,90.
#
# Space ends the pause and the casts play out: the Rays and Larloch's
# fly as travelling projectiles, Magic Missile and Ice Dagger fly at
# the goblin, the cones land north of the party. The rolls (damage,
# the strength drains) are past the pass gate: only experience points
# and enemy hit points are compared.
#
