# What the original does here (sandbox recordings, seed 1):
# - The party (one character, Probe, at 2414,897 on the Wicked Wench's deck)
#   arrives inside Hedron Kerdos' opening dialog (10HEDRON, speaker 1048592,
#   entry 0, 3 responses); the game waits there with no time limit. The
#   shortest way out is entries 0 -> 1 -> 17 -> 18 -> 19 (Continue) -> 20 ->
#   end, about frame 2420.
# - Hostiles never reach the party: the goblins of AR1000 stay where they
#   are through the whole scenario (party hp 9 of 9, creatures 36 at every
#   dump); nothing here goes into known_differences.json.
# - A creature is picked by its selection circle (CGameSprite::IsOver tests
#   the animation's ellipse around the feet), so the NPC click is on Hedron's
#   circle, after a move so the AI update has picked him.
# - The view does not follow the party and edge scrolling did not move it,
#   so the walk west is in legs: a double click on the portrait
#   (CInfGame::OnPortraitLDblClick) centres the view on Probe, which puts
#   Probe at screen 400,214 or so, and the next leg is clicked relative to
#   that. Each leg ends on the point clicked (dumps: 2104,1002; 1784,966;
#   1352,846; 1032,977).
# - Every unlocked door is in the west of the area (AR1000_Door3 to Door6);
#   the nearest, AR1000_Door5 (the house at 823..868,870..930, walk-to point
#   846,978, the third door in dump order), is about 1,500 pixels from the
#   ship. The locked AR1000_Door1 and Door2 are as far, so there is no
#   door_locked checkpoint.
# - On the way Reig Redwaters (10REIG, speaker 983055) sees the party at
#   about 1667,949 and starts his dialog himself (entry 0, 2 responses); it
#   runs 0 -> 2 -> 4 -> Jon's 2 (speaker 786444) -> 5 -> 6 -> 12 -> 13 -> end.
#   Hedron's dialog is therefore before the door, not after it.
