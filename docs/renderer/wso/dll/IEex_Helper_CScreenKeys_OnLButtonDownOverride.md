##### Behavior

Caller: the staged `IEex_Gui_Patch.lua` line 1467 — hooks vanilla
`CScreenKeys::OnLButtonDown` at 0x639790.

1. Marks the engine input object: `*(root+0x1c50)+0x9fe = 1`.
2. `DAT_10094464(x, y)` (cursor set) and `DAT_1009445c(0)` (select the
   keys panel).
3. Translates the click into panel space: x -= panel+0x24, y -=
   panel+0x28.
4. With no dialog open and no cell selected: hit-tests the 60 page cells
   against the column controls (0x10000041+i for the action column,
   0x10000005+i for the key column; rect fields +0e x, +0x12 y,
   +0x16 w, +0x1a h) and selects the hit page-relative row with
   `DAT_10094278(i)`.
5. Forwards the translated point with `DAT_100944d8(x, y)`.

##### Failure fallbacks

None; a miss simply selects nothing.
