The IME composition with Double Byte Character Support (CImm::sub_4D89E0, sub_7C2CC0, sub_7C2E10, sub_7C3020); with Ctrl: End and Home (the original sets the top line to the text's length, which AdjustVisibleIndex corrects), word jumps left and right over \n\r and spaces, copy (Ctrl+C and Ctrl+Insert, which drops the selection), paste (Ctrl+V), cut (Ctrl+X); without: Backspace and Delete (a soft break goes with the character beside it; the last character goes without a reflow), Enter (a hard break), Escape (restores the saved text and drops the capture), Space, Page Up and Down and Up and Down (the column nearest the cursor's by pixel width), End, Home, Left and Right (stepping over soft breaks), Shift+Insert (paste), Shift+Delete (cut), F3, F4 and VK_PROCESSKEY (the IME's suggestion frame), letters with the capitalization modes, digits and keypad digits with the shifted symbols (0x8ABA48, 0x8ABA54), the keypad operators and the punctuation keys (0x8ABA3C with 0x8ABA24 and 0x8ABA30).
