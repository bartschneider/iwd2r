## Notes

Ported (the known spells panel's automatic pick button,
CUIControlButtonCharGen61A130::OnLButtonClick at 0x61A2CA). From spell level
1 to field_14A3 it fills m_nExtraSpells known spells: a specialist wizard
(GetSpecialization() & 0x4000 clear) whose school has a spell at the level
(sub_618D10) and who knows none gets one picked at random (rand() % count)
from the usable wizard spells of the school, removing the spell at index 1
first when no pick is left; then spells come in the order of
SPLAUTOP.2DA (CRuleTables::sub_545EB0) when usable, not spwi402, not
excluded by the specialization and not known. Each added spell selects its
button (controls 2-25). The next level's count is 4 (the byte table at
0x853B34) plus 1 for a specialist (GetSpecialization() & 0x3FC0, the table at
0x853B40); the original's branches for class numbers 5 and 19 (0x618B6D,
0x618BDE) read the bard and sorcerer known spell tables and are kept as
written though no IWD2 class has those numbers there. The sprite is taken
from the character creation engine's m_nGameSprite (g_pBaldurChitin + 0x1C64,
then + 0x4E2), as the original does. Exempt from an oracle case: it needs a
sprite and the UI; the world_entry fighter does not reach it.
