## Notes

The dithered horizontal line (16-bit, mirrored): every other pixel of
[xMin, xMax], starting on the pixel the row's parity picks. After the
optional one-pixel skip the original counts `(width + 1) >> 1` pixels
(objdump: `inc` then `shr 1`, after a `jle` past an empty line); ours
counted `width / 2`, so a line of odd length lost its last dithered pixel.
On the "unallocated points" prompt of scenarios/chargen_replay.txt that was
the screen's last column (x 799) on every other row, dithered in the
original's screenshot and not in ours.

The same fix in all six dithered variants (16, 24 and 32 bits, plain and
mirrored), each checked in the disassembly; only the 32-bit plain one is
drawn in that scenario.

No oracle case: it draws into a locked surface.
