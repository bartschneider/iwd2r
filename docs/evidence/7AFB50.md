## Notes

Ported from the Ghidra listing and the disassembly (0x7AFB50-0x7AFEBF, `ret 0xc`): the brightest blit
of a whole frame - the 8-bit frame's indices through the realized 16-bit palette
(`CVidImage::rgbTempPal`, one WORD a pixel), the transparent index skipped, rows bottom-up by
`lPitch` in bytes, and every drawn pixel is the per-channel brighter of the destination and the
palette through the mode's masks (`Rmask`/`Gmask`/`Bmask` at 0x18/0x1C/0x20, read at 0x7AFB88-0x7AFBAA).
It is the 16-bit twin of `Blt8To32Brightest` (0x7D0730) and the sibling of T59's `sub_7AF1C0` and
T66's `sub_7AF8A0`: same palette setup (`GetFrameData` first, then `SetPalette` unless
`m_bPaletteChanged`, then `Realize(rgbTempPal, 16, dwFlags, &m_paletteAffects, 0xFF)`), same
`lPitch - width * 2` byte row step, and with `!m_bShadowOn` only the low word of
`rgbTempPal[SHADOW_ENTRY]` takes the mode's `field_24` (0x7AFC40). Called by `CVidCell::Render`
0x7AE5D0 only (the 0x8 branch). NOTE: the survey (report-T50.md) names it `Blt8To16Brightest`; the
export has only the address, so the code keeps the stub's `sub_7AFB50` and the Render call site
stays untouched.

The max is `max(dest & mask, pal & mask)` ORed over the three masks (0x7AFD22 and 0x7AFE39): the
channels are disjoint, so the order does not matter. The compressed walk is the compiler's
two-pixel unroll with a token lookahead (it writes pairs as one DWORD), which decodes the same
per-row run walk as the 32-bit template; the port keeps the per-row form. Unlike the 32-bit
sibling's compressed branch there is **no** `(palette & 0xF0F0F0) != 0` guard here: every drawn
pixel takes the max (raw and compressed alike).

Checked against T59's findings: neither of that port's 16-bit-only walk quirks is here - the raw
walk reads `[esi]` (0x7AFE32, no one-byte lead) and the walks continue with width-based conditions
(`jne`, and `je`/`jbe` against the remaining width), never the `ja`-after-`dec` carry, so no early
row end. The original has **no zero-size guard**: its do-while loops would spin on an empty frame
(Render never passes one), so the port returns TRUE there with a NOTE.

## Oracle

`vid/cell_brightest16` (oracle/cases/vid.cpp): a synthetic BAM (zeroed `CResCell` with the parsed
flag, header, palette and one frame entry; data raw or RLE), random frames up to 8x4 with the
transparent index mixed in and index 1 present, the palette flags, the cell's shadow on and off and
the mode's shadow colour, drawn at (8, 8) of a 64x64 16-bit canvas of random bytes, on 5-6-5 and
5-5-5 in turn (the realization and the max read the mode's masks). The whole canvas and the result
are compared. The unit test (`tests/test_vid.cpp`, "brightest16") pins the walk, the per-channel max
against the masks (including the two hardcoded spot values) and the zero-size guard on both layouts.
