# 0x5CB930 CInfTileSet::SetResID

## Why ours was kept

The batch row flags WillScarlettOhara's fork (iwd2-re@e8dd16a750966af53f24daaf49dcb4fb13594c13,
read through git plumbing) as changed against our base body. Diffing his body
against ours shows the only differences are one member rename, used three times: he stores to ``m_pResTiles[index]->m_cResRef`` (and the dual-tile variant) where we store to ``field_58``..

``m_cResRef`` does not exist in our CResInfTile header; both name the
tile's resref copy at +0x58.

## The exe reading

objdump 0x5cb930..0x5cbc00: ``5cbb44: lea 0x58(%edi),%ecx`` and
``5cbb5d: lea 0x58(%edi),%ecx`` immediately before the two thiscall
CResRef::operator= calls - both resref copies land at +0x58 of the freshly
constructed CResInfTile, which is what our ``field_58 = cResRef`` writes.

Ours already matches the exe; adopting his naming would need header edits
outside this batch's owned files. Kept as ours.
