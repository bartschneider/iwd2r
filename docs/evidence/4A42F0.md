## Notes

Pending: ours not implemented, and needs engine state. The case is
registered with ORACLE_CASE_PENDING and has no body.

Identified from IEex's name (CGameEffect_CheckSave), now named in
tools/ghidra/extra_labels.txt. IcewindMisc::Roll (0x5845D0) has only the three
callers in Tenser's Transformation, so saves were looked for among the
callers of rand (0x7E8160) and CUtil::UtilRandInt (0x780C80). The save dice
are not rolled here: CGameSprite::ProcessEffectList (0x72DE60, see
72DE60.md) rolls the four d20s into the sprite at 0x70F6-0x70F9, and this
function receives pointers to them.

Calling convention (objdump): `__thiscall`, the effect in ecx (0x4AA0AE
`mov ecx, edi` before the call at 0x4AA0B0), five stack arguments, one
`ret 0x14` at 0x4A4AF2, BOOL (a byte) in al. Derived signature:
`BOOL CGameEffect::CheckSave(CGameSprite* pTarget, BYTE* pFortitudeRoll?,
BYTE* pReflexRoll?, BYTE* pWillRoll?, BYTE* pRoll5)`; the bytes' bit 0x80
marks a roll already displayed. The order of the three saves follows the
effect's flags at +0x3C (4, 8, 0x10) and the save bonuses read
(+0x93C, +0x93E, +0x940 of the sprite); the names are not confirmed.

What it does (TRUE: the effect applies): returns TRUE early for some
effect types and when the game's +0x43E6 is 1; for a spell-resistance check
(flags at +0x98 and +0x58) it compares the fifth roll plus the effect's
+0xC4 and +0xC8 with the target's +0x94A and displays string 0x4B18; otherwise it adds the roll the flags
select to the matching save bonus, plus trap sense, racial, feat, difficulty
(+0x4456 of the game) and school bonuses, and compares the sum with 10 +
the effect's fields +0x14 and +0x40. A natural 20 saves and a natural 1
fails. CGameSprite_DisplaySaveString (0x765830) reports it.

Why no body: the first instruction past the prologue reads the global at
0x8CF6DC (g_pBaldurChitin) and its +0x1C54 (the game). It then calls the
target's virtual GetAIType (vtable +0x10), its feats (0x763150), class
levels (0x762760, 0x447990), and through CGameObjectArray::GetShare the
effect's source (flag 8) and the party (flag 0x10). Faking a running CInfGame and a sprite with a
vtable goes beyond R11b.
