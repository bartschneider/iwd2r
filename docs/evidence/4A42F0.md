## Notes

Ported (stream effects-save, M3) as the virtual CGameEffect::CheckSave, the
vtable slot +0x18 every effect class but CGameEffectDamage keeps (its
override is 0x4A9E30). Callers: CheckAdd (0x4A3310, through the vtable at
0x4A3E75, with the sprite's four rolls), CGameEffectDamage's override (direct
call at 0x4AA0B0) and CGameEffectSlipperyMind::OnRemove (0x4A5EF0, through
the vtable at 0x4A5F57, with copies of the rolls). A scan of every
`call [reg+0x18]` in .text with five pushed arguments finds only those two
virtual sites.

Calling convention (objdump): `__thiscall`, five stack arguments, `ret 0x14`
at 0x4A4AF2, BOOL in eax: TRUE applies the effect, FALSE refuses it (saved,
resisted or excluded). The four BYTE pointers are, in order, the fortitude,
reflex, will and spell resistance rolls (the sprite's +0x70F6..+0x70F9);
bit 0x80 of each marks it as shown, and the low seven bits are the d20.

What it does, in order:
- race 190 (the target's AI type +6 == 0xBE) applies at once;
- in a cutscene (game +0x43E6, m_gameSave.m_cutScene, == 1): the target's
  own effect and effect 104 apply, a death applies only with its +0x18C
  (CGameEffectDeath::field_18C), anything else is refused;
- poison (25), and the poison portrait icon (142 with flags 6), are refused
  while the target has mirror images (only the count's low byte, 0x4A43D0
  `test al,al; ja`) or stone skins (> 0);
- spell resistance, for a hostile (source flags 0x400) effect with m_flags
  bit 1 and not bit 2: the derived resistance (+0x94A, halved for effect
  451); unless the resistance roll is already shown, FeedBack(83, SR, roll,
  caster level, 39673, penetration, 0) (shown only with the
  EnableRollFeedback INI option); when SR is above caster level (the byte at
  +0xC4) + penetration (+0xC8) + roll (an unsigned compare, 0x4A44AA), the
  effect is refused, and if the roll was not shown yet the line 19224
  ("resisted", with the source's name from 0x5864F0) is displayed, the flags
  lose their low two bits and the roll is marked;
- no saving throw bits: applies;
- DC = 10 + m_saveMod + m_spellLevel; each of the bits 4 (fortitude), 8
  (reflex) and 0x10 (will) sets the roll and the bonus (save + roll),
  overwriting the previous bit's, so with several bits the last one decides;
  fortitude against poison adds 2 for a dwarf and the Snake Blood (2) and
  Resist Poison (4) feats; reflex adds trap sense
  (CRuleTables::GetTrapSenseBonus of the target's derived stats) when the
  source is a trap (type 0x41, its share taken in the SHARED/DENIED loop);
  will adds 4 against SPWI420 (a 7-byte compare) for a party-side target
  within 100 of a living party member with two paladin levels (the party
  share loop waits for SUCCESS with no other exit, 0x4A4896), 2 for a
  halfling against fear (24) and 2 for a drow (the target's own m_typeAI at
  +0x26/+0x27, not the virtual GetAIType); each bit marks its roll shown
  and, if it was not shown before, asks for the save line;
- then +5 with the creature's base flag at +0x89F bit 1; outside the party
  (EA != PC) the difficulty (game +0x4456) 1 takes 4 and 2 takes 2 (the INTs
  at 0x84EB04, 0x84EB08); elves and half-elves +2 against enchantment
  (school 4), gnomes +2 against illusion (5), dwarves +2 without m_flags bit
  2, monks of level 3 or more +2 against enchantment, a specialist +2 against
  their own school;
- saved when the roll is 20, or the total reaches the DC and the roll is not
  1; the save line (0x765830) is shown when a roll was first shown here.

NOTE: without a fortitude, reflex or will bit (but a nonzero saving throw)
the original decides on a dword whose low byte is the spell resistance roll
and whose upper bytes were never written (0x4A436C stores only the byte);
ours takes them as zero.

No rand: the dice come from CGameSprite::ProcessEffectList (0x72DE60) and
AIUpdate. The Catch2 tests pin the generator's state unchanged across every
call.

Found on the way: CGameSprite::sub_763200 (0x763200, the feat prerequisites
sub_763150 ends with) has no case for Resist Poison (the original's at
0x7636E9) and returns FALSE for Snake Blood (0x7637AE), so those two poison
bonuses do not apply in ours yet; CGameSprite.cpp is the vis-probe stream's.

Why the case stays pending without a body: the function's first instruction
past the prologue reads g_pBaldurChitin (0x8CF6DC) and its game (+0x1C54),
and on every path it calls the target's virtual GetAIType (vtable +0x10);
the reflex and will branches share objects from the game's object array and
the save line goes through the options, the talk table and the message
screen. An honest fixture would need an original CGameSprite from its own
constructor (R10a: a loaded CRE through the resource manager) and a running
CInfGame; R10b allows only zeroed blocks the function reads, not faked
engine globals or vtables. Ours is covered by tests/test_effects_save.cpp
([checksave]): the DC and both bounds of each save, the natural 20 and 1,
several bits, the racial, school, flag and difficulty modifiers, the
paladin's aura, spell resistance (first and shown rolls, 451), the cutscene
rules, the poison guards, and Hold Person (SPPR208) through AddEffect on a
goblin from 00GOB.CRE, saved and failed.
