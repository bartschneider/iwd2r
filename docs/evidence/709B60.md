## Notes

Port checked and completed. The visual effects loop (32 bits at derived
+0x10C, 0xA2C) matched except that every branch that picks a new sequence
at the end of one (LAB_0070A30F: shroud of flame's random sequence, the
fire shields' 2, effects 28/29's 3, entangle/web/fear's next, the default's
random) also rewinds to frame 0 (CVidCell::FrameSet(0)); ours did not.

disassembly (Ghidra mislabels the stack slots): with the splash finished and
the particle arrays allocated, CalculateFxRect (animation vtable +0x04) gives
the effect rectangle (esp+0x3C) and reference point (esp+0x2C). The row
(game time >> 1) % sequence length starts again: its slots are cleared to
0xFFFF and, while the duration is at least the sequence length, base + rand()
% random intensity particles get rand() % sequence number as their sequence
and a random position in the middle third of the width and the lower four
fifths of the height. The other rows' particles move on (their counter
increments) and are put back into that box when they left it (rand() again),
the first such move saving the rectangle and point (+0x5308, +0x5318). The
duration counts down each tick. The intensity base is a signed byte
(StartSpriteEffect sets -2).
