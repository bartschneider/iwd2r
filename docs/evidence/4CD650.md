# 0x4CD650 CGameTrigger::AddEffect

Override of `CGameAIBase::AddEffect` on `CGameTrigger`. Applied effects are
dispatched on the effect id: DETECTTRAPS arms the trap-found message chain
(`CMessageSetTrigger` with CAITRIGGER_DETECTED, `CMessageTriggerStatus`,
the 0x40cca0 sprite lookup with `AutoPause(0x80)`, and the draw-poly 400
refresh), CASTSPELL turns the effect into a FORCESPELL `CMessageAddAction`
aimed back at the trigger, and SUMMON spawns the creature named by the
effect's resref at the nearest open grid square and attaches the timed
removal effect. Every dispatched effect object, and any other id, ends at
`delete pEffect`.
