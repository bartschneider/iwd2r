# 0x4E2B50 CUIControlTextDisplay::RemoveAll

## Disassembly reading

- The critsect at +0xA8E (s_csSharedList), the delete loop over the
  string list and the post-unlock zeroing of m_posTopString,
  field_5A, field_5C, m_posHighlightedItem and field_A6C are the same
  sequence ours performs.
- The panel-rect invalidate and the control invalidate close the
  function in the same order.

## Notes

The fork delta is the member renames (field_A8E to s_csSharedList,
field_5A to m_nTopIndex, field_A6A to m_nVisibleLines and the line
counters), declared in the control header this batch does not own.
Ours is kept as the exe-faithful body.
