## Notes

Stops the night ambient, refreshes m_sndAmbientDayVolume (+0x986) from
m_headerSound.m_dayAmbientVolume (day plan at this+0x94, extended-night
variant at this+0x8C), and - unless the day ambient is already playing, in
which case it only re-applies the scaled volume - re-resolves the day
ambient resref per GetMemoryAmount and plays it looped on channel 1 at
m_sndAmbientVolume * m_sndAmbientDayVolume / 100. Both resref arms join at
LAB_00478128 before the shared play tail. Ends with CInfinity::SetDay when
(this+0x624) & 2.
