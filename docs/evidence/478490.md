## Notes

Unlike SetDay/SetNight, dawn scales instead of replaces: it writes
m_sndAmbientDayVolume (+0x986) = m_headerSound.m_dayAmbientVolume *
nIntensity / 256, and if the day ambient is not already playing and the
combined volume is nonzero, re-resolves the day resref (plan at this+0x84,
extended-night variant at this+0x8C) and plays it looped on channel 1.
Both arms join at LAB_00478723; an already-playing ambient only gets
SetVolume. The same ramp then runs for the night ambient
(m_sndAmbientNightVolume +0x988, plan this+0x98, extended this+0xA0),
joining at LAB_004789F5. At nIntensity == CInfinity::TRUE_DAWNDUSK_INTENSITY
it removes the music position for GetSong(0) and plays song slot 0 with
flags 0x4|0x2, then relays CInfinity::SetDawn(nIntensity,
bPlayDayNightMovie) when (this+0x624) & 2; the ambient and
already-playing paths all converge at LAB_00478A59 before that tail.

## WSO delta

The two bodies already matched structurally, so the adoption is carried by
details rather than shape: his body keeps the song id in a `char` (ours
declared `BYTE nSong` for the same GetSong(0) call feeding
RemoveMusicPosition(const CResRef, INT)), and his SetChannel calls cast
the area pointer to DWORD. Compile fix only: those two casts became
`reinterpret_cast<DWORD_PTR>(this)` - the build is 64-bit with -Werror
(CMakeLists.txt:22), where the narrowing pointer cast is a hard error, and
SetChannel's second parameter is DWORD_PTR (src/CSound.h:27). Adopted his
body with those fixes.
