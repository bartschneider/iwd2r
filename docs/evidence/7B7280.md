## Notes

The plain 16-bpp software blit of the classic renderer (CVidBlitter::Init
selects it only at `m_nBpp == 16`, and only when the Soft options are on;
iwd2r itself runs at 32 bpp, so nothing reaches it in play - ported for the
record). The destination rect is the source rect's size at the destination
point; `SoftLockSurfaces` (0x7B6B20) locks both surfaces into the blitter's own
DDSURFACEDESC blocks (+0x06 destination, +0x72 source) and keeps their
`lpSurface` in +0xE2/+0xDE; the rows then copy `2 * width` bytes with the row
step `lPitch` (the pitch kept in bytes, `>> 1` for pixels), honouring a
`DDBLT_DDFX` request's mirror flags: left-right through the dword-swapping row
copy at 0x7B6CC0 (the 16-bit pixels in reverse), up-down by starting the source
on its last row and walking it backwards. A `DDBLT_COLORFILL` request goes to
0x7B7070 instead. The original ends with two `Unlock` calls on the
destination surface (the second with the source's pointer) and never unlocks the
source surface; kept as it is, both are no-ops in the shim.
`tests/test_ddraw.cpp` pins the plain copy, both mirrors and both at once on two
shim surfaces (the 16-bit lanes are the first `2 * N` bytes of each row), and
the lock's refusal for a destination rect off the surface.
