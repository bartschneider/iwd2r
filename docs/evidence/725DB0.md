so the drain-side loop calls sub_725CC0 with **field_8 - v1**: the count
to remove is what the entry holds above the level's new remaining
slots, and every entry's field_8 lands on v1 (the shared-slot sorcerer
model). The twin add-side 0x725D30 passes **v1 - field_8** (0x725d6f:
`mov %ebp,%ecx; sub %edx,%ecx`) — the asymmetry is by design.

Our port had the drain side as `v1 - field_8` (unsigned wrap →
new f8 = 2*f8 - v1, field_10 collapsed to 0), inherited from the fork.

never re-armed, and digit keys stopped switching sprites. Fixed to
`m_List[nIndex].field_8 - v1` (src/CGameSpriteSpells.cpp sub_725DB0).
