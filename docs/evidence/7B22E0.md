## Notes

Ported from the decompile and disassembly; was a stub returning FALSE. Unreachable in iwd2r: the DirectDraw shim lists only 32-bit modes (platform/ddraw_shim.cpp FillModeDesc) and GetDeviceCaps reports 32 bits, so CVideo never selects 16 or 24 bpp; ported for the record.

Writes pixel pairs as one DWORD from the low words of rgbTempPal: width / 2 pairs a row (an odd last pixel is not drawn), the destination stepping by pitch / 2 - width words a row. With 0x8 the rows are dithered with the video mode's field_24: even rows keep the first pixel of a pair and put field_24's low word in the second; odd rows put field_24 in the first and OR it whole into the pair (or ebx,[esp+0x10] at 0x7B23A1), so its high word is ORed into the second pixel. NOTE kept.
