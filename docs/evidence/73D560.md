## Notes

Ported in full (src/CGameSprite.cpp, `CGameSprite::Damage`). Signature from
Swing's calls: `CGameEffect* Damage(CItem* curWeapon, CItem* pLauncher, SHORT
nAbility, BOOL bCritical, const CAIObjectType& targetType, SHORT
nTargetDirection, SHORT nAttackDirection, CGameSprite* target, BOOL
bOffHand)`. It returns a new CGameEffectDamage (vtable 0x84AE3C, built inline
from CGameEffect's default constructor; ours uses CGameEffectDamage(), which
sets the same id 12 and m_special -1): the amount is the damage as a SHORT,
the flags the damage type, m_special the weapon's attributes
(LoadWeaponIdentification, +0xA).

Rand draws (pinned by tests/test_combat.cpp): one UtilRandInt(dice, luck) per
die of the ability (none under Kai, spell state 64: dice times count), one
per die of the launcher's ability, the sneak attack dice (rand() % dice per
roll), and the poison of a coated weapon (two rand() % 6). The fallback
ability is DEFAULT_ATTACK (0x85BBD0; Ghidra shows it as the string "\x01").

Sneak attack (0x73D85F-0x73DC26): the direction test is written out from the
disassembly (Ghidra's goto web): a party attacker (EA up to GOODCUTOFF) whose
direction is within four of the target's, or a flat-footed target (helpless
or GetMoveScale 0, the animation's vtable +0x40), checked against uncanny
dodge.

NOTE: The original adds the 16-bit bonuses zero-extended; ours adds them as
WORDs too. Only the low word is used (the minimum, the critical multiplier
and the effect's SHORT amount).

NOTE: For a damage type of 0 or above 9 the original leaves the effect's
flags at the sneak attack damage (the switch has no default); reproduced.
