# 0x533F40 CProjectileFollowUp::Fire

## Decompiled (anchored on the exe; driver bundle empty)

The driver bundle for 0x533F40 carries no decompile, no callers and no
callgraph entry, so this import is anchored on the exe bytes (see
exemption). What the exe does, in order:

1. Stores `source` into +0x72 (`m_sourceId`), `target` into +0x76
   (`m_targetId`), both point dwords into the CPoint at +0xC8
   (`m_targetPos`, `lea ebx,[esi+0xc8]`), and zeroes the WORD at +0x1DC
   (`m_nDirection`).
2. Copies its effect list into the child's: walks the inlined MFC list (head
   pointer at +0x82), calls each effect's virtual `Copy`, stamps the copy's
   +0x9C slot number with the child's WORD projectile type (zero-extended
   DWORD store), and `AddTail`s it onto the child's list at +0x7E.
3. Registers itself with the game object array (`Add(&m_id, this,
   INFINITE)`); on anything but the SUCCESS byte it destroys itself
   (`delete this`, virtual slot 0 with arg 1) and returns.
4. Otherwise moves itself into the area with `CGameObject::AddToArea(pArea,
   &m_targetPos, height, LIST_FRONT)`.
5. Plays its fire sound through the member at +0xEE — Stop, the CResHelper
   `SetResRef` (a standalone call here, not inlined as in 0x533AE0), the
   looping flag when the +0x15A flag is set, channel 15 bound to the
   pre-update `m_pArea`, then `Play(m_pos.x, m_pos.y, 0, FALSE)`.
6. Stores the new area, hands the child its call-back id, records the launch
   height (+0x2EE) and clears its ready flag (DWORD +0x2AE), then fires the
   child through virtual slot +0x6C with its own seven arguments.
7. Decodes a replacement child of one type higher (`DecodeProjectile(m_childType
   + 1, NULL, 0)` with a 16-bit increment), copies the spell level (+0x186)
   and the source resref (+0x18A) into it, decrements the WORD shot counter
   at +0x2A0, and reloads the WORD delay at +0x2F8 from the interval at
   +0x2A2.

## Notes

`ret 0x1c` — six arguments plus the two-dword CPoint. MSVC reloads the
arguments into ebx/edi after the calls that would otherwise clobber them
and reuses argument home slots as scratch: the nType home holds the
spilled `m_loopFireSound` across the sound block, and the point.x home
serves as the dead second destination of the 0x78AC30 resref copy — both
unobservable. The `SetChannel` in the sound block reads `m_pArea` before
this function stores the new area; the port keeps that order (PlaySound
before `m_pArea = pArea`). The child's Fire runs before the replacement
child is decoded, so the first launch carries the constructor-decoded
child. The DWORD-sized ready flag and the WORD fields (shots, interval,
child type, delay) are all confirmed from the AIUpdate head at 0x534140,
which reads the same offsets from the other side (0x53416b, 0x53417b,
0x534195) — AIUpdate itself is not a row of this batch.
