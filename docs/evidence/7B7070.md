## Notes

The `DDBLT_COLORFILL` form `SoftBlt` reaches: the destination rect (or, with
no rect, the whole surface) locked with the same retry as 0x7B6B20 and filled
with the DDBLTFX's fill colour, its low word in both halves of each 32-bit
store - two 16-bit pixels a dword - with the same HRESULT mapping and one
`Unlock`. With no destination rect the original asks the surface for its size
through `GetSurfaceDesc` (vtable +0x58), which our shim does not have; the port
locks the whole surface with a null rect instead, the same rectangle, and takes
the size from that description.
