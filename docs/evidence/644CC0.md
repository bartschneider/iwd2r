# 0x644CC0 CUIControlButtonMapAreaMap::SetMap

## Disassembly reading

- Locking and capture: the exe constructs CSingleLock over the critical
  section at this+0x73A and locks it, reads cResMap from
  pArea->m_pResWED->GetResRef() and stores m_pArea = pArea - ours the
  same, unlocking at the end.
- Night suffix: the exe strips the night map suffix through a CString when
  the area type's 0x40 bit is set, then FindKey(cResMap, 1004, TRUE) picks
  the branch; on hit (and a changed m_vmMap.cResRef) SetResRef with
  m_bDoubleSize from the globals and field_71E = TRUE; on miss
  SetResRef(empty), m_bDoubleSize = FALSE, field_71E = FALSE - ours
  matches both arms.
- Portrait loop: m_nCharInArea = 0, then six iterations of GetShare
  (0x599a50) with the retry over the SHARED/DENIED status bytes; SUCCESS
  fills m_charPositions[nPortrait].ptPos/id and counts up, any other
  result stores -1/-1/INVALID_INDEX; ReleaseShare (0x599e70) runs on both
  outcomes - ours writes exactly this.
- Notes block: with m_vmMap.pRes non-null the exe builds the field_72A
  rect from GetInfinity()->nNewX/nNewY and rViewPort behind two OffsetRect
  guards (the exe's `nAreaX - right - 1 + left < left` is algebraically
  the guard ours writes), then IntrnlInitialize, the
  m_cGameAreaNotes.m_rArea store, field_7BC = TRUE, SetActiveNotes(TRUE),
  InvalidateRect; otherwise SetActiveNotes(FALSE) and InvalidateRect -
  ours matches.

## Notes

The pre-exported decompile renders this loop's GetShare retry as
DENIED-only; the raw pattern for the same retry elsewhere (0x641270's loop
at 0x641549) compares both status bytes (SHARED at 0x851870 and DENIED at
0x851871), which is the shape ours writes.
