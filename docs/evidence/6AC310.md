## Notes

Ours had no Render override: the vtable slot fell back to CGameAnimationType::Render 0x55D170, which draws nothing, so every creature with an animation ID 0x7xxx that MonsterOld::NewSupport 0x6AF4A0 accepts (0x7F01 mind flayers, 0x7F0A panthers, 0x7F13 snakes, 0x7F07 golems...) was invisible.

Read from the disassembly (the parameters at [esp+0x40] after the three pushes, then +4 after `push ebp` at 0x6AC459):

- No MIRROR_BAM test: 0x6AC398 compares m_currentBamDirection (0x7C4) with the BYTE m_extendDirectionTest (0x897) as signed words and ORs in MIRROR_FX (the DWORD at 0x85193C, 0x10); then always FXPREP_COPYFROMBACK (0x851934, 0x80) | 4.
- m_translucent (0x7CC) adds 2 and makes transparency (transparency * 128) / 255 + 128 (the 0x80808081 multiply), 128 when it was 0; otherwise 2 only when transparency != 0.
- The tint: each of R (low byte), G and B is the cell's plus rgbTintColor's minus 255, 0 when negative (`setl`, `dec`, `and`). The same for the weapon cell (0x5E2) when m_renderWeapons (0x88E) is set. NOTE: the 14 Renders ported earlier take red from the blue bytes (GetBValue in their r line); this one follows the original.
- field_7D0 set: the CResBitmap of field_7D4 (its pRes at 0x878) is demanded and its colour table set as the cell's palette (SetPalette(table, count, the WORD at 0x85E848, TYPE_RESOURCE 0)), released after the draws.
- FXRender of the body, then of the weapon cell (same reference point, flags and transparency); the weapon's tint, then the body's, are restored.
- FXRenderClippingPolys with the bounds raised by posZ, as the other Renders.
- FXUnlock at 0x6AC6FA: bFadeOut (0x6C after the push) set passes &rFXRect and ptPos + ptReference; otherwise 0x6AC6E9 pushes `eax` (0) as the rectangle and &CPoint(0, 0), the NULL the 14 Renders were fixed to pass.
- FXBltFrom(nSurface, rFXRect, ptPos, ptReference, flags).
