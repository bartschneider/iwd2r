## Notes
Ported in the round 4 fix round of the ui-misc stream (ruling M5-19), from
the disassembly (0x5C4AD0-0x5C72C7). The host's transition of the whole
party through a party required region. Arguments: the character, the
region, the area name LeaveAreaName read (used for field_1B86 and the
clients).

Order, as the original:
1. The visible area's 0x47A190; nothing without a connection.
2. The world screen back (from the area map; for the host from any other
   screen), the sound list cleared, CWeather::OnAreaChange(TRUE).
3. The host: the auto-save (AUTO_SAVE_NAME, the screen grab flags
   field_366E/50D8/50DC and SaveGame(TRUE, FALSE, TRUE), the 3D flag
   tested twice as in LeaveAreaName). NOTE: 0x438F90, the message to the
   other players, is not ported.

   field_1B86 = the area name, the progress screen (9891, 9885) with the
   target 11000000 for the host (6000000 otherwise), the host's
   listen-to-join option saved and cleared.
5. The region's area and entry names (read to the NUL by the original;
   bounded, P4-10); without the region nothing more (the progress screen
   stays, as the original).
6. The host counts the players and waits up to 40 rounds of 60 ms for
   the others' resource demands; alone it does not wait.
7. LoadArea(area, 0xFF, FALSE, TRUE): LoadArea's own rand() rolls when it
   loads.
