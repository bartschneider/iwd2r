# 0x50C260 CMessageSpriteUpdate::CMessageSpriteUpdate

## Why ours was kept

The batch row flags WillScarlettOhara's fork (iwd2-re@e8dd16a750966af53f24daaf49dcb4fb13594c13,
read through git plumbing) as changed against our base body. Diffing his body
against ours shows the only differences are four member renames on CDerivedStats: his ``m_nACArmorBonus``/``m_nACNaturalBonus``/``m_nACDeflectionBonus``/``m_nACDodgeBonus`` for our ``field_6``/``field_8``/``field_A``/``field_C``..

His names do not exist in our CDerivedStats header; both spellings
read the same four halfwords at derived-stats +6/+8/+A/+C into our message's
``field_64``/``field_66``/``field_68``/``field_6A``.

## The exe reading

Bundle decompile: ``in_ECX[5] = *(undefined4 *)((int)param_1 + 0x556e);
in_ECX[6] = *(undefined4 *)((int)param_1 + 0x5572);`` - two dword loads from
the sprite's embedded derived stats (+0x556E = stats+6, +0x5572 = stats+A)
covering exactly those four halfwords, coalesced into paired moves. Same four
fields ours writes individually.

Ours already matches the exe; adopting his naming would need header edits
outside this batch's owned files. Kept as ours.
