## Notes

Checked against the disassembly (0x558310-0x55846B). The colour given
(the area's light after ApplyBrightnessContrast, from
CInfinity::PostRender 0x5CEDE0) is scaled channel by channel by
CSnowFlake::SNOWFLAKE_BASE_COLOR (0x84EE8C, RGB(230, 230, 230)):
blue `imul; sar ecx, 8` (0x55837A), green the product's high byte
(`mov dl, ch` at 0x5583AA), red `imul; sar eax, 8` (0x5583B6). The
viewport of the visible area's infinity (+0x514 of the area) is locked
with BKLock (0x79DD50). Each of the first m_nCurrentDensity flakes (0x36
bytes apart) gets the colour at +6 and is drawn by BKRender (0x79E060)
with CParticle::DOTS (0x85EB62) and blob size 5. Then BKUnlock
(0x79DDE0).

Fixed: ours multiplied without the shift and kept the low byte (the RGB
macro). Under white light that drew the flakes (26, 26, 26) where the
original draws (229, 229, 229). Under AR1000's light the walk_and_talk
`door_open` shot now has the original's (202, 202, 202) flakes (225 of
them where the original has 221). Their positions still differ: that is
the flakes' update (CSnowStorm::AsynchronousUpdate 0x557FC0,
CSnowFlake::AsynchronousUpdate 0x558590 and their rand draws), not this
render.
