## Notes

The 32-bpp path was mostly ported; differences from the original fixed:

- The pulse of a range's tint, add and light is offset by 1 << range: the original divides (1 << range) + m_nAUCounter, not m_nAUCounter, by 2 * period (0x7BFBFB, idiv). Ours left the offset out.
- An add with period 0 or 1 sets the add percentage to the period (local_b4 = period); ours kept the previous value.
- A pulsing light adds 8 to each channel ((c * pct) / period + 8); ours did not. The range light channels are dwords in the original; ours stored them in an RGBQUAD, which would wrap at 256.
- A range without a light keeps the previous range's light percentage; ours reset it to 0.
- With suppressed tints the ratio is rgbTint + pct * (nTintMax - rgbTint) / period; ours added to an uninitialized value.
- Range channel arithmetic follows the original's types (unsigned division for the add and light, signed for the tint).

The 16-bpp branches stay as they were.

Fixture (oracle/cases/vid.cpp): the original's video mode is a CVidInf from its constructor (0x79AF00), since SetType calls GetTransparentColor through the vtable; g_pChitin and the engine are zeroed blocks holding only pActiveEngine, the 3d flag and the AU counter; the palettes come from the original's constructor (0x7BEEA0) with m_nAUCounterBase set on both sides (otherwise the counter starts from the palette's address). Inputs: 256 random entries, every flag bit RealizeRange and its callees read, per range tints, adds and lights absent, steady or pulsing (period 2 to 30), suppress masks, fade, brightness, gamma and global tint; all 256 destination entries compared.

Fix round 1: the range channel locals are initialised to 0 (read only when their pointer is set; quiets -Wmaybe-uninitialized) and the green-key comparison is unsigned, as the original's.
