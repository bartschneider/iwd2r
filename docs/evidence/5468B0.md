# 0x5468B0 CRuleTables::CheckUseMagicDevice

level is the item's max effect spell level; scrolls (item type 0xB) look
up the spell named by the resref up to the first Z/z and use its level
when loaded and nonzero. The roll is the (signed) UMD skill plus
`rand() % 20 + 1`, succeeding at `nLevel + 20`, and is reported through
FeedBack with the 0x9988 tag after a discarded sub_763E40 call on the same
