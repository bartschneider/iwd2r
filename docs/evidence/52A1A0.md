## Notes

Ported as CProjectile::DeliverEffects. Waits for a share of the target (retrying SHARED and DENIED). A target that is not an AI object (type & TYPE_AIBASE, 0x84C501) gets nothing: the effects are deleted. A sprite (TYPE_SPRITE, 0x84C507) immune to the projectile type (its derived stats' CImmunitiesProjectile at sprite+0xAA8, OnList 0x4E7120) or, for a spell level (the low byte of +0x186) below 9, to that spell level (CImmunitiesSpellLevel at sprite+0xAFC) gets nothing either, and CGameEffect::FeedBackImmuneToResource (0x4C4330) names the source resource (+0x18A). Otherwise every effect is wrapped in a CMessageAddEffect (vtable 0x8487CC; caller the source +0x72, target +0x76), the list is emptied and the messages go to the handler as one CMessageGroup (0x5152C0). Tested by tests/test_projectile.cpp (message group, immunity sections).
