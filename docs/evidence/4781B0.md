## Notes

Stops the day ambient, refreshes m_sndAmbientNightVolume (+0x988) from
m_headerSound.m_nightAmbientVolume (night plan at this+0x98, extended-night
variant at this+0xA0), and - unless the night ambient is already playing, in
which case it only re-applies the scaled volume - re-resolves the night
ambient resref per GetMemoryAmount and plays it looped on channel 1 at
m_sndAmbientVolume * m_sndAmbientNightVolume / 100. Both resref arms join at
LAB_004783F8 before the shared play tail. Ends with CInfinity::SetNight when
(this+0x624) & 2.
