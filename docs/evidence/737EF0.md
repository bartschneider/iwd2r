## Notes

New port (`CGameSprite::Attack`, the Attack action's handler, IEex's
CGameSprite_Attack). Checks the target (in flight, invisible or in sanctuary:
TARGETUNREACHABLE; dead: attack the nearest enemy; sleeping and the fist:
only a monk), the weapon (usable, charges), shows "Attacks" once per target,
starts the battle music (not for MGEN, MKG1-3), the battle cry (sound 5),
and walks to the target (MoveToObject 0x73EDD0) until the reach (the
ability's range, the larger with a launcher, plus the personal spaces) and
the line of sight allow a swing (0x739B60).

The ExecuteAction case for ids 3 and 98 (0x7290DB-0x72920D) shares the
target and calls it; see 728F80.md.
