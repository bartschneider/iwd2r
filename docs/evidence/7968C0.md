## Notes

`CVidMode::DrawLightingEffect16` (0x7968C0, `ret 0x18` at 0x7977C6): the
16-bpp light pool. Same skeleton as the 32-bit sibling (`DrawLightingEffect32`,
0x7CAC60): the scale starts at 64 and a pixel is scaled by it >> 6 (.rdata
0x85DE38/0x85DE3A), the ambient is tinted by `rgbGlobalTint` (+0xA4), each
channel brightens to the light plus half the ambient's shortfall and is
rounded down to a multiple of the height above the base, the caps are drawn
from `GetEllipseArcPixelList` through the vtable (0xC0), then the middle
band. The 16-bit differences:

- each channel is taken through the mode's masks `m_dwRBitMask`/`G`/`B`
  (+0x18/+0x1C/+0x20) and **clamped at the mask's value**, not 255
  (`cmp ecx, edx; mov [esp+0x58], edx` at 0x796BDF etc.), then re-masked and
  OR'd (`0x796C24-0x796C3A`);
- two pixels are written per 32-bit store: the second uses the doubled
  deltas (`bVar7 + cVar5 + cVar5` in the middle-band pairs, e.g. 0x7971xx),
  an odd first/left pixel is peeled out of the pair loop;
- `lPitch` is in pixels (the dispatcher passes `ddsd.lPitch >> 1`);
- the middle band's down ramp is **nWidth - 1** pixels (0x797191 `dec edx`),
  where the 24-bit sibling ramps nWidth and the 32-bit one shifts.

Quirks kept as the siblings document theirs: the pixel list is allocated
before the `cy == 0` test (the original leaks it, 0x7968E6), the original
leaks it on the all-zero-channel return, and the band divides by a width the
arc can shrink to zero - ours stops drawing and frees the list.

The oracle case `vid/lighting_effect16` runs 5-6-5 and 5-5-5 masks with
randomised canvases, lights, ambients and tints against the original; the
unit test in tests/test_vid.cpp pins the pool's middle, its edges, the
untouched outside and the two empty returns.
