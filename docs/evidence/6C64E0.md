# 0x6C64E0 CGameAnimationTypeCharacter::EquipArmor

## Why ours was kept

The batch row flags WillScarlettOhara's fork (iwd2-re@e8dd16a750966af53f24daaf49dcb4fb13594c13,
read through git plumbing) as changed against our base body. Diffing his body
against ours shows the only differences are one expression rewrite: he builds ``CString((char)field_144C, 1)`` and concatenates that, where our body appends ``field_144C`` (CHAR) directly with ``operator+(const CString&, char)``..

Both compute resref + armorChar, but the call sequences differ: his
constructs a CString(TCHAR, int) temp and then uses the CString+CString
concatenation; ours uses the single-char overload. The exe uses the single-char
overload, so our body is the faithful one. His explicit form would compile to a
different helper chain than the exe executes.

## The exe reading

objdump 0x6c65ab..0x6c65cc: ``mov 0x144c(%esi),%dl`` (the armor char
from +0x144C), ``push %edx``, a CString copy ctor (``call 0x7fa83c``), then
``call 0x78a970`` - CString::operator+(const CString&, char) taking the raw
byte. No CString(TCHAR,int) construction appears. Our compat declares exactly
that overload (compat/mfc_string.h:156), which ``m_resRef + m_armorCode +
"G1" + field_144C`` resolves to.

Ours already matches the exe; adopting his naming would need header edits
outside this batch's owned files. Kept as ours.
