## Why ours was kept

read through git plumbing) as changed against our base body. Diffing his body
against ours shows the only differences are five renames (``field_4814``/``field_487C``/``m_gameSave.field_1B0``/``field_4AB2`` and the ``sub_5A0160`` call) plus one added statement pair - ``memset(m_gameAreas, 0, sizeof(m_gameAreas)); m_visibleArea = 0;`` after ``m_pGameAreaMaster = NULL;``..

``m_soundNeedParty``/``m_soundAreaTransitionRefused``/``m_nGroupInventoryNumber``/
``m_bTriggerOutline``/``ValidateCache`` do not exist in our headers). The added
memset is a different matter: the exe never writes the game-area array here.
