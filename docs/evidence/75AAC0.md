## Fork comparison (rule 1)

WillScarlettOhara's body (`CAIAction.cpp` @ e8dd16a7, lines 216-229) sets the same
five fields, but renders the `*this` initialization as a scoped local
(`{ CAIAction action; }`) — the decompiler dropped the ECX link. Kept literally, his
body would leave `m_actorID`/`m_acteeID`/`m_acteeID2` and `m_string1`/`m_string2`
uninitialized (UB once Berserk's `operator=` copies them). Fixed on our side by
delegating to `CAIAction()` first; adopted his field assignments verbatim. Rule 3
adaptation, noted here and in the commit message.
